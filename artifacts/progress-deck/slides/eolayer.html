Excluded: no MWIR band (CBERS-4, ECOSTRESS, LWIR-only constellr and Hydrosat); not operational (NOAA-15/18/19, Meteor-M 2-2); commissioning (8 OroraTech OTC-P1).

Public means government or intergovernmental mission; private satellites form the commercial layer. Fire-capable means a MWIR fire band, or high-resolution SWIR with an active-fire method. A public fire product only decides validation, not membership: S1 keeps the satellites that can be checked against the public record, so S1 against S0 stays clean. FY-3D joins S1 because a public global fire product exists; its August 2025 data need an NSMC account (requested). Data access differs: open for Landsat, Sentinel-2 and Metop; registration for FY-3; restricted for HJ-2 and Kanopus-V-IK.
