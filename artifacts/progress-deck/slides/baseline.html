Answers the meeting question on the time from declared ignition to the first observation, from public data. Larouco ignited just after the afternoon pass burst and waited 4.83 hours for the next Terra pass; Molezuelas ignited minutes before an afternoon VIIRS pass. No pass missed the fire before T0, so the delay is entirely waiting for a pass. Gap statistics use a common horizon of 47.7 hours after T0. Each platform updates the fire roughly twice a day, so the worst holes come from the orbit schedule. These are the targets the central-node architecture must close.
