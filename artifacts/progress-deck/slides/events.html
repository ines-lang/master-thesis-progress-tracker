Global FIRMS screen of 8 to 15 August 2025 with five criteria: vegetation fire, onset inside the window, at least 500 ha in FIRMS, spreading fire, northern hemisphere (Asia: Siberia and Far East). Candidates are ranked by official final area, as the two Spanish events were in Month 2; FIRMS area cannot rank them because it misses burning after 15 August. Piódão and Derdara have no official ignition hour, so their T0 reference is the first SEVIRI detection. First FIRMS is the first detection after the reference time.
