Proposal: freeze the ground segment as the S1 stations (Svalbard, McMurdo, Poker Flat for stored data; TDRSS for Terra; Madison, Hampton and Mayaguez for direct readout over the US) and make every satellite added in S3 and S4 downlink only to them. Only satellites that broadcast their data directly can use the direct-readout stations. Reason: comparability; with the same stations in every scenario, the difference between scenarios is due to the space segment. The stations are not open to everyone: Svalbard and Troll belong to KSAT, a commercial operator renting antennas to space agencies; McMurdo, Poker Flat and TDRSS are NASA's; the direct-readout stations belong to universities and NASA. Using them for other satellites is plausible but a modelling choice. Each satellite's real segment is kept as a sensitivity case, with no new run, since ground links are filtered in post-processing. Checked candidates: Sentinel-3 sends science data to Svalbard only (ESA); the US direct-readout set matches NASA's FIRMS ultra-real-time stations, though the network has grown (5 own and 15 partner stations in 2026); EUMETSAT's EARS network relays VIIRS data in Europe, but not as a public fire product, so it is a candidate fast path for S1 to S4, not part of S0. Open: the stations of the S2 coordination layer.
