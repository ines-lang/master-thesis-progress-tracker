Every S1 pass over the six events from the reference time to +24 h, elevation mask 10 degrees, Terra excluded because its downlink goes through the TDRSS relays. The Month 2 set was optimistic: Suomi NPP and Sentinel-3 downlink only at Svalbard and often wait a full orbit. US direct-readout stations, which feed the FIRMS ultra-real-time product, receive Red Canyon passes while the satellite still sees the fire; no such fire path exists for Europe or Morocco. A slower, realistic baseline avoids overstating the gain of the central nodes in S2. Sources: NOAA OSPO notices, ESA Sentinel-3 data flow, NASA Aqua interface document, NASA Earthdata FIRMS URT.
