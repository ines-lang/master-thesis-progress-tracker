Same link rule for all sensors

Downlink to ground, or RF crosslink to a central node in view. No relay links.

What stays fixed

Coordination layer, ground stations, detectability filter per sensor.

The extended public layer and the commercial layer are handled the same way: each joins the network with the same link rule as the EO layer and is added on top of the previous scenario, so S3 against S2 isolates the public sensors that do not share data today, and S4 against S3 the private ones. Sensor limits enter through the detectability filter: fire band, day or night use (Metop AVHRR fire band at night only; Landsat and Sentinel-2 daytime only), and pixel size against fire size. Commercial parameters are partly from press releases and are reported as ranges.
